# Legacy Equine genetics reference

The game models documented inheritance rather than treating color as a random label. At every locus, a foal receives one randomly selected allele from each parent. A heterozygous × heterozygous recessive cross therefore has 25% affected, 50% carrier, and 25% clear outcomes; a dominant variant's displayed probability follows the actual parental allele counts.

## Primary references

- UC Davis Veterinary Genetics Laboratory, [Horse Coat Color](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/resources/horse-coat-color): base-color, dilution, white-pattern, Gray, Roan, Leopard Complex/PATN1, Silver/MCOA, and lethal-white summaries.
- UC Davis VGL, [Lethal White Overo](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/lethal-white-overo): Frame Overo inheritance and the 25% O/O risk from two N/O parents.
- UC Davis VGL, [Dominant White](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/dominant-white-horse): tested KIT variants and variants reported or suspected to be homozygous embryonic lethal.
- UC Davis VGL, [Arabian Genetic Tests](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/services/horse/arabian-genetic-tests): CA, LFS, SCID, and OAAM.
- UC Davis VGL, [Horse Embryo Preimplantation Genetic Diagnosis](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/horse-embryo): GBED, HERDA, HYPP, MH, PSSM1, CA, LFS, and coat loci.
- Arabian Horse Association, [Anglo-Arabian registration rules](https://arabianhorses.org/export/content.export/aha-handbooks/2020-handbook/CHAPTER10.pdf): Arabian/Thoroughbred ancestry and the 25–75% Arabian range.
- AQHA, [foal registration guidance](https://helpcenter.aqha.com/knowledge/-how-do-i-register-a-foal-with-aqha): Appendix eligibility from an AQHA horse crossed with a registered Thoroughbred.
- International Quarab Horse Association Italia, [Quarab standard](https://www.quarab.it/english/): Arabian with Quarter Horse/Paint ancestry and the 1/8–7/8 range.

## Scope and safety

The database distinguishes non-viable genotypes from inherited diseases that can produce a living horse. A lethal draw is never represented as a live foal and all economic/cooldown changes roll back. This is a game simulation, not a substitute for veterinary genetic testing. Variant definitions and breed rules are data-driven and should be revised when primary laboratories or registries publish updated evidence.
